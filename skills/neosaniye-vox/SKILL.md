# NeoSaniye Collage Factory

1. Write a factual 40-45 second story with 8-12 visual beats.
2. Give every beat one dominant subject, one short headline and a detailed image prompt.
3. Generate a complete 9:16 historical/editorial collage for every beat.
4. Use torn paper, archival photography/illustration, halftone grain, aged cream stock, charcoal, dark red, mustard and dusty blue.
5. Do not put readable text inside generated images; Remotion adds headlines and labels.
6. Use provider fallback chains. Missing keys, quota errors and temporary failures move to the next provider.
7. Remotion performs restrained push, pan, parallax-style framing, paper wipes, music, voice and selective SFX.
8. Workflows are manual-only and upload-free.
