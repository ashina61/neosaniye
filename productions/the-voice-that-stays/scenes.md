## The two takes

s08 and s09 are the film. They are the same narration voice, RMS-matched to
each other and to the rest of the film **on purpose** — if the bone take were
also louder, the viewer would hear "louder", which is not the claim. Same
loudness, different half of the spectrum, twenty-five seconds after being told
what to listen for.

Measured in `sim/mix.py`, and gated there: bone carries +9.0 dB more under
320 Hz, air carries +9.2 dB more over 4.5 kHz. If either gap closes the build
stops, because two demos that sound the same turn the last twenty seconds into
a man asserting something.
