# Art Direction — The Bulge Nobody Can Explain

Atelier piece. Hand-authored for this subject only. Checked against
`productions/STYLE_LEDGER.md` before any of it was designed; every field below
differs from the previous production on purpose.

## The design read

**A page from a physics book, being corrected in front of you.** Not an
instrument reporting a measurement — a hand showing its working. Warm, patient,
made of paper and ink. The drawing keeps its construction lines. When the video
contradicts a textbook it does so without relish, and the wrong annotation stays
on the page struck through rather than erased, because the viewer learned it in
good faith.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#EDE6D6` | The ground. Warm, faintly grained. Never white. |
| Ink | `#141210` | Every line of the drawing. |
| Water | `#1F4E6B` | Sea blue. Used for water and for nothing else. |
| Force | `#C1452F` | Vermilion. **Every red mark on screen is a force.** No exceptions. |

Four values, no gradients, no fills except water. Construction lines are ink at
low opacity, not a fifth colour.

## Type

- **Spectral 600** for language — the closing caption is set like the caption
  under a figure, not like a title.
- **Spectral 400** letterspaced for the few labels.
- **Caveat 600** for exactly one thing: the textbook margin annotation that gets
  struck out. It never appears again.

No monospace anywhere. This is a page, not a readout.

## Motion character

Ink draws on and **settles with a small overshoot**, the way a hand draws.
Nothing in this video moves at constant velocity — which is precisely how the
previous production moved, and the reason it is forbidden here.

`visual_variance: 6` · `motion_intensity: 4` · `information_density: 4`

## The signature device

**Three arrows of unequal length**, drawn along the Moon's axis. They are the
entire explanation and they need no label. They appear in exactly two beats —
sc-04, where they are established, and sc-06, where the middle one is subtracted
and the two bulges fall out as arithmetic. Scarcity is what makes sc-06 land.

## The ending

**The video does not stop.** The Moon at the right margin is still drifting
outward when the cut comes. The previous production ended on its only motionless
frame; ending on unresolved motion is the deliberate inverse.

## Anti-patterns

- Anything from `how-headphones-erase-sound`: dark ground, cyan/amber signal
  pair, a running counter, waveforms on a grid, a still final frame.
- Monospace type.
- Photographic space imagery or a realistic Earth.
- Mocking the textbook diagram.
- A centred caption block over b-roll.

## Quality gates

1. A viewer with the sound off must reach the answer from the arrows alone.
2. Vermilion may only ever mean force.
3. The last frame must still be moving.
4. No layout may be recognisable from the previous production.
