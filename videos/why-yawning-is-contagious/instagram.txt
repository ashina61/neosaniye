Someone yawns, and seconds later so do you. You had no say in it.

The oxygen story is wrong — your blood was never short of it. Spontaneous yawning starts before birth, but catching one only appears around age four or five. That gap is the giveaway: contagion is learned social wiring. It scales with closeness, and chimpanzees and dogs do it too.
