The tiny hole in airplane windows isn't a flaw; it's a vital safety feature.

That small hole in an airplane window serves a critical safety function. It's a 'bleed hole' that equalizes pressure between the outer and middle panes, ensuring the outer pane bears the full pressure load. This design creates a crucial backup: if the outer pane ever fails, the middle pane is ready to take over and maintain cabin pressure.
