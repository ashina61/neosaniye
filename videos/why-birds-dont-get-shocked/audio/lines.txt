A bird lands on a live power line. Thousands of volts. Nothing happens.
Electricity isn't hunting the bird. It's trying to reach the ground.
Current only flows where there's a difference in voltage.
Both feet on the same wire. Both feet at the same voltage.
No difference. No push. No current.
The bird is a detour to nowhere, so the charge stays in the copper.
But touch a second wire, or a grounded pole, and that difference appears instantly.
Which is why the biggest birds are the ones at risk.
