Ever wondered why metal feels colder than wood at the same temperature? It's all about how quickly materials conduct heat away from your skin!

It's a common misconception that metal objects are colder than wood objects when they've been in the same room. The truth lies in thermal conductivity! Metal is a much better conductor of heat than wood, meaning it pulls heat away from your skin faster. You perceive this rapid heat loss as 'coldness', even though both materials are at the same ambient temperature. It's the rate of heat transfer, or flux, that tricks your senses.
