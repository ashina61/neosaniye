Hiccups are a common and often annoying phenomenon, but what actually causes them and why do they eventually stop?

Hiccups occur due to an involuntary reflex arc. First, the diaphragm spasms, pulling air into the lungs. Immediately after, the glottis snaps shut, creating the characteristic 'hic' sound. Hiccups stop when the brain's voluntary control overrides this reflex arc.
