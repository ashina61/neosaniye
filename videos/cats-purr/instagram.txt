Cats purr at a frequency between 25 and 150 Hertz, but it's not always a sign of happiness.

A neural oscillator in a cat's brain sends rhythmic signals to the larynx. This causes the vocal folds to vibrate, producing the purring sound. While often associated with contentment, cats also purr when stressed or injured, suggesting it serves multiple functions beyond just happiness.
